## Phase 6: Operational Safety Modules (Workplace Incident Investigation & Electronic Permit to Work)
1. **Workplace Incident Investigation & 5-Why Root Cause (ISO 45001 §10.2)**:
   - Standardized incident reporting across classifications (Near Miss, First Aid, Medical Treatment, LTI, Environmental Spill, Major Catastrophe).
   - Interactive 5-Why causal tree and Fishbone / Ishikawa root cause analysis workflows.
   - Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) tracking with due dates, hierarchy assignments, assignees, and close-out sign-offs.
2. **Electronic Permit to Work (e-PTW) System (ISO 45001 §8.1.2)**:
   - High-hazard work permits (Hot Work, Confined Space Entry, Critical Lifting, Working at Height, Electrical LOTO).
   - Atmospheric multi-gas testing interlocks (O2, LEL, H2S, CO) with mandatory re-test timer.
   - Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) isolation certificates and multi-tier approval chains.
   - Interactive Live Permit Board with active permit counters and expiration alerts.
